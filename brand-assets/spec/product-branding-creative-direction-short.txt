Haechi Product Branding and Creative
Direction Document

1. Brand Name: Haechi (해태)
Origin and Meaning: Haechi (해태, Haetae) is a mythical creature from Korean folklore. It is
typically depicted as a lion-like beast with a horn on its head, often associated with justice,
protection, and the ability to distinguish good from evil. In traditional Korean architecture,
Haechi statues are placed at the entrances of palaces and temples to ward off evil spirits
and disasters, particularly fire. The creature symbolizes vigilance, integrity, and
guardianship.
Brand Essence: Haechi embodies Vigilant Protection, Unwavering Integrity, and
Adaptive Intelligence in the digital realm. It represents a guardian that not only defends
against threats but also upholds the principles of privacy and justice in an increasingly
complex digital landscape.
2. Brand Personality
 • Protective: A steadfast guardian, always watching over its users' digital well-being.
 • Intelligent: Sophisticated, leveraging advanced AI and cutting-edge technology to stay
   ahead of threats.
 • Trustworthy: Reliable, transparent, and committed to ethical practices and user
   privacy.
 • Modern & Innovative: Forward-thinking, constantly evolving to meet new challenges
   with creative solutions.
 • Empowering: Gives users control over their digital lives, making complex security
   accessible.
 • Mysterious (subtle): Hints at hidden depths and advanced capabilities, without being
   intimidating.
3. Brand Tone of Voice
 • Authoritative yet Approachable: Confident and knowledgeable in cybersecurity, but
   communicates clearly and simply, avoiding overly technical jargon when addressing
   general users.
 • Reassuring & Calm: Conveys a sense of security and peace of mind, especially when
   discussing threats.
 • Forward-Looking: Emphasizes innovation and proactive defense.
 • Ethical & Principled: Reinforces the commitment to privacy and digital rights.

4. Visual Direction (Artistic & Creative)
4.1. Core Visual Metaphor: The Digital Guardian
The Haechi creature itself serves as the primary visual metaphor. It should be reinterpreted
for the digital age, blending traditional symbolism with modern, sleek aesthetics.
4.2. Color Palette
 • Primary Colors:
    • Deep Midnight Blue (#0A1128): Represents security, trust, depth, and the digital
      night sky where threats lurk.
    • Electric Teal (#00C4B4): Symbolizes innovation, technology, vigilance, and a
      beacon of light/protection.
    • Charcoal Grey (#333333): Provides a sophisticated, stable, and professional base.
 • Secondary/Accent Colors:
    • Subtle Silver/Light Grey (#CCCCCC): For highlights, subtle gradients, and clean
      typography.
    • Deep Violet/Purple (#6A0572): Used sparingly for emphasis, representing
      intelligence and mystery.
4.3. Imagery & Iconography
 • Abstracted Haechi Symbol: A stylized, minimalist representation of the Haechi
   creature, focusing on its horn and protective stance. It should feel modern, geometric,
   and strong, not overly literal or cartoonish.
 • Digital Grids & Networks: Visual elements that evoke interconnectedness, data flow,
   and secure pathways.
 • Light & Shadow: Use of contrasting light and shadow to convey depth, protection, and
   the idea of illuminating hidden threats.
 • Shields & Barriers: Abstract representations of protective barriers, digital force fields,
   or layered defenses.
 • Subtle AI/ML Visuals: Abstract patterns or subtle animations that hint at intelligent,
   adaptive systems without being overtly robotic.
4.4. Typography
 • Headings: A strong, modern sans-serif font (e.g., Montserrat, Lato, or similar) that
   conveys authority and clarity.
 • Body Text: A highly readable sans-serif font (e.g., Open Sans, Roboto, or similar) that is
   clean and professional.
 • Monospace (for code/technical elements): A clean monospace font (e.g., Fira Code,
   Source Code Pro) to denote technical precision.
4.5. Motion & Interaction (for digital interfaces)
 • Smooth Transitions: Fluid and subtle animations that convey sophistication and
   efficiency.
 • Responsive Feedback: Clear visual feedback for user interactions, reinforcing control
   and security.
 • Dynamic Visualizations: Abstract, real-time visualizations of data flow or threat
   detection (e.g., subtle pulsing lights, shifting patterns) that are informative but not
   distracting.
5. Messaging Pillars
 • Unbreakable Privacy: Emphasize the multi-layered, AI-driven protection that makes
   user data virtually untraceable.
 • Intelligent Defense: Highlight the adaptive nature of the VPN, constantly evolving to
   defeat new threats.
 • Empowering Control: Focus on giving users and businesses the tools to take charge of
   their digital security.
 • Trust Through Transparency: Reinforce the open-source foundation and ethical
   commitment.
 • Seamless Security: Position the product as powerful yet easy to use, integrating
   effortlessly into daily digital life.
6. Target Audience Perception
 • For SMBs: Haechi should be perceived as a reliable, advanced, and comprehensive
   security partner that protects their business and their clients, enhancing their own
   trustworthiness.
 • For Consumers: Haechi should be seen as a personal digital guardian, providing peace
    of mind, ultimate privacy, and freedom in the online world.
This document serves as a guiding star for all creative endeavors, ensuring a cohesive and
impactful brand presence for Haechi across all touchpoints. The goal is to communicate
strength, intelligence, and unwavering protection in a visually compelling and trustworthy
manner. This document will be further elaborated into specific briefs for brand and web
designers.
