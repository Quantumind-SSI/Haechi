Haechi Brand Designer Guidelines

1. Brand Overview
Haechi is a next-generation secure communications platform designed for enterprises,
government contractors, and critical national infrastructure (CNI) operators. It provides
verifiable, provably anonymous, and quantum-resistant network communications,
addressing the converging threats of quantum decryption, advanced traffic analysis, and
centralized trust architectures. The brand embodies Quantum-Resilient Protection, Zero-
Knowledge Integrity, and AI-Driven Stealth.
2. Core Visual Metaphor: The Invisible Shield
The primary visual metaphor for Haechi is the Invisible Shield. This concept should guide
all design elements, representing advanced cryptography, quantum resilience, and stealth.
The visual identity should convey an impenetrable, adaptive barrier against sophisticated
digital threats. Avoid literal or cartoonish depictions; instead, focus on abstraction,
precision, and a sense of advanced technological defense.
3. Color Palette
The official Haechi color palette is designed to reflect the serious, high-stakes nature of
enterprise and government security, utilizing high contrast to convey precision and
alertness.

4. Typography
4.1. Headings
 • Font Family: A strong, geometric sans-serif font (e.g., Inter, Space Grotesk, Syncopate).
 • Purpose: Conveys technical authority, modern engineering, and structural integrity.
   Should be clean, impactful, and highly legible.
4.2. Body Text
 • Font Family: A highly legible, clean sans-serif font (e.g., Roboto, Open Sans).
 • Purpose: Optimized for dense technical information, reports, and data dashboards.
   Ensures readability across various digital and print mediums.
4.3. Monospace
 • Font Family: A crisp monospace font (e.g., JetBrains Mono, Fira Code).
 • Purpose: To be used for cryptographic keys, code snippets, terminal interfaces, and
   technical data points, emphasizing precision and technical accuracy.
5. Logo Design Guidelines
5.1. Concept
The logo should abstractly represent the
concept of an "Invisible Shield" or "Digital Guardian" with a focus on quantum resilience
and zero-knowledge principles. It should be modern, minimalist, and convey strength,
security, and advanced technology without being overly aggressive or complex.
5.2. Elements to Incorporate (Abstractly)
 • Haechi Abstraction: A highly stylized, geometric representation of the Haechi creature,
   focusing on its protective stance or horn, but rendered in a way that suggests digital or
   cryptographic elements rather than a literal beast.
• Shield/Barrier: Abstract forms that suggest protection, an impenetrable barrier, or a
  secure enclosure.
• Quantum/Digital: Subtle hints of quantum mechanics (e.g., superposition,
  entanglement) or digital networks (e.g., interconnected nodes, data flow) integrated
  into the design.
• Stealth/Invisibility: Visual cues that imply hidden strength, seamless integration, or
  undetectable operation.
5.3. Color Usage in Logo
• The logo should primarily utilize Dark Charcoal or Deep Black as its base, with Vibrant
  Orange-Red used sparingly for emphasis, a focal point, or to represent an active state
  of protection/alertness. White should be used for contrast or to define key shapes
  within the logo.
• Consider monochrome versions for versatility across different backgrounds and
  applications.
5.4. Prohibited Elements
• Avoid overly literal or cartoonish depictions of the Haechi creature.
• Do not use gradients that compromise clarity or readability.
• Avoid overly complex designs that do not scale well or lose impact at small sizes.
• Do not use bright, distracting colors outside the defined palette.

6. Brand Accessories & Applications
6.1. Digital Interfaces (Web, App, Dashboards)
• Layout: Clean, dark-themed interfaces with high contrast. Utilize Dark Charcoal and
  Deep Black for backgrounds, White for primary text, and Vibrant Orange-Red for
  interactive elements, alerts, and key data points.
• Iconography: Minimalist, line-based icons that align with the geometric typography.
  Icons should be clear, concise, and communicate function efficiently.
• Motion: Subtle, smooth transitions and animations that convey sophistication and
  efficiency. Dynamic visualizations of data flow or threat detection should be abstract
  and informative, not distracting.
6.2. Marketing Materials (Presentations, Brochures, Whitepapers)
 • Design: Professional, clean, and data-rich. Maintain a consistent dark theme with high-
   contrast text.
 • Imagery: Use abstract, high-resolution imagery that reflects cryptography, secure
   networks, and advanced technology. Avoid stock photos of generic people or offices.
 • Data Visualization: Charts and graphs should adhere to the brand color palette, using
   Vibrant Orange-Red to highlight key metrics or trends.
6.3. Physical Branding (Merchandise, Signage)
 • Consistency: Ensure the logo and color palette are consistently applied across all
   physical assets.
 • Materials: Consider premium, durable materials that reflect the brand's commitment
   to quality and security.
 • Subtlety: For merchandise, subtle branding (e.g., embossed logos, monochromatic
   designs) may be preferred to maintain a sophisticated and exclusive feel.
7. Imagery Guidelines
 • Abstract & Conceptual: Focus on imagery that evokes concepts of security, data,
    networks, and advanced technology rather than literal representations.
 • High Contrast: Utilize light and shadow effectively to create depth and highlight key
    elements.
 • Modern & Futuristic: Imagery should convey a sense of innovation and forward-
    thinking, aligning with Haechi's future-proof technology.
 • No Stock Photos of People: The brand focuses on technology and security
    infrastructure, not human interaction. Avoid generic stock photography featuring
    people.
This document serves as a comprehensive guide for brand designers to ensure consistency
and impact across all visual touchpoints for Haechi.
